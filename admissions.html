Frequently Asked Questions

What are the admission requirements?

Admission requirements vary by program. Generally, we require a completed application form, previous academic records (if applicable), and a placement assessment. For younger students, we conduct developmental assessments to ensure proper placement.

Is financial aid available?

Yes, we offer limited financial aid and sibling discounts for families in need. Please contact our admissions office to discuss financial assistance options and application procedures.

What is the student-teacher ratio?

We maintain small class sizes to ensure personalized attention. Our average student-teacher ratio is 12:1, with even smaller ratios for specialized programs and younger age groups.

Do you offer transportation services?

Currently, we do not provide transportation services. However, we can help connect families in the same area for carpooling arrangements. Our location is easily accessible by public transportation.

What are the school hours?

School hours vary by program. Regular academic programs run from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM, while evening and weekend programs are available for Quranic and Islamic studies. Specific schedules are provided upon enrollment.
