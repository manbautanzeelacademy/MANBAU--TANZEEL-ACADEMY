Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses and programs

What is the class schedule for courses?

Class schedules vary by course and student level. We offer flexible timing including weekday evenings, weekends, and online options to accommodate different schedules. Specific timetables are provided upon enrollment.

Are there any prerequisites for enrolling in courses?

Most beginner-level courses have no prerequisites. For intermediate and advanced courses, we conduct placement assessments to ensure students are appropriately placed for optimal learning.

Do you offer financial aid or scholarships?

Yes, we offer limited financial assistance and sibling discounts for families in need. Please contact our admissions office to discuss available options and application procedures.

Can I switch courses after enrollment?

Course transfers are possible within the first two weeks of the term, subject to availability and placement assessment. Please contact our academic advisors for assistance with course changes.

What materials are required for courses?

Required materials vary by course. Most courses include textbooks and materials in the course fee. Digital resources are provided through our student portal. Specific requirements are detailed in the course syllabus.
